The neon-blue timer at the top of the HUD flickered: 0:45.

"They're heavily anchored on 2F Big Tower," Sledge called out over comms, his voice tight over the static. "We're running out of time. We have to rush 2F Site right now."

Blitz didn't hesitate. He led the charge up the White Stairs, the heavy thud of his tactical boots echoing off the timber-framed walls of Oregon. Behind him, Ash, Zofia, and Sledge stacked close, non-verbal coordination forged through hundreds of rounds.

At the top landing, Blitz hit his flash shield with a blinding white pulse, instantly catching a roaming Jäger who was holding a tight angle on the corridor. Before the Defender could clear his vision, Ash burst past with her R4-C flared, putting him down with three crisp shots.

"Dorm Main is clear! Go, go, go!" Ash barked.

They breached 2F Kids Room in a sudden storm of dust, flashbangs, and splintering wood. Smoke grenades slammed into the doorways, turning the narrow hallway into a thick fog bank. A Lesion Gu mine hit Blitz's leg with a painful sting, but he pushed through the agony, planting his shield firmly in front of the doorway to absorb the inevitable hail of bullets.

A volley of gunfire erupted from the back of Trophy Room. Smoke grenades masked the sightlines, but bullets tore through the drywall. Zofia immediately fired a concussion grenade into the corner, interrupting the defender's spray. Taking advantage of the momentary suppression, Sledge swung his sledgehammer in a massive arc, pulverizing the soft wall to the objective in a single, devastating blow.

"Breached! Plant, plant, plant!"

Suddenly, a razor-sharp Kiba Barrier kunai slammed into the wall right beside Ash's head. Instantly, the expanding bulletproof compound didn't block the attackers—it completely sealed off the room's doorways from the outside, trapping the Attackers in the site while locking her own surviving team out.

Over the defender comms, Azami's cold voice cut through the static: "The objective is sealed. Let the gas do its work."

Azami wasn't securing the site for her team; she was playing the long game. A remote-activated Smoke canister, hidden inside the room before the rush, began hissing thick yellow toxins into the enclosed space. The Attackers were caged, but so was the objective.

"She trapped us in here!" Sledge yelled, frantically swinging his hammer against the hardened Kiba barrier, but it barely dented.

With ten seconds on the clock and their lungs filling with smoke, Ash scrambled over the defuser, fingers flying over the keypad while their health bars plummeted in the dark.
